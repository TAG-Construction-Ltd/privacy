Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your TAG Connect account and the personal data TAG Construction Limited holds about you.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

How to delete your account

TAG Connect is provided by TAG Construction Limited. You can delete your account in either of these ways:

  1. In the app. Open TAG Connect and go to Profile → Settings → Delete my account. We send a 6-digit code to confirm it's you, then you confirm the deletion.
  2. By email. If you can't use the app, email support@tagconstructionltd.co.uk with the subject line "Account deletion request". Include your full name and the phone number or email address registered to your TAG Connect account so we can check it's you.

We review your request and schedule your account for deletion. You can cancel within 7 days. We'll confirm by email when it's complete.

What is deleted

  • your profile, photo and skills
  • your card and certificate photos
  • your login

If you have never worked for TAG, everything is deleted within 7 days.

What the law makes us keep

If you have worked for TAG, some records must be kept for a set time after you finish working with us. We keep only what the law requires, and delete it when that time is up.

DataRetention periodReason
Right to work check 2 years after you finish working with us Immigration law
Pay records As long as HMRC requires Tax law
