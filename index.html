TAG Connect · Privacy Policy

Your data, your control

This policy explains how TAG Construction Limited collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use the TAG Connect app.

Version 2.0 · Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version

  • We are TAG Construction Limited. We look after your information. ICO registration ZA389594.
  • We hold your ID, your cards and your work record so you can prove you are qualified and get sent to work.
  • Your profile is private. A company sees it when you apply to them or are sent to them. Nobody else.
  • We never sell your data. We do not advertise to you. We do not track you across other apps.
  • The app does not use your location. We use the postcode you type in, nothing more.
  • A person, not a computer, makes every decision about your documents and about work.
  • You can see, correct, download or delete what we hold — and complain to us, or to the regulator.

This is a summary. The rest of this policy is the full detail.

4 · What we collect

You give us

  • your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, address and National Insurance number
  • photographs of identity documents — passport, driving licence, share code or residence permit
  • a photograph of yourself, taken when you verify your identity
  • your qualification cards and certificates — CSCS, CPCS, NPORS and others
  • health-related certificates where the work needs them — occupational health, drug and alcohol tests, medicals, face-fit, eye and hearing tests
  • your work history, trade and the jobs you have done
  • your emergency contact
  • messages you send recruiters in the app

We create or record

  • your site attendance, placements and site diary entries
  • your Verified status, your Skill Score and your Activity (section 8)
  • sign-in records, the type of device you use and the app version, to keep the service working and secure
  • a notification token for your device, so we can send you alerts

We get from other places

  • documents a recruiter uploads for you — for example a card you handed in at the office
  • your details from TAG’s own records if you have worked for TAG before (section 5)
  • the town and approximate area for the postcode you type in

Two things worth knowing. Your emergency contact is someone else’s information — please check they are happy for you to give it. And when a recruiter uploads a document for you, it appears in your wallet and is treated exactly as if you had uploaded it yourself.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for or use your phone’s location. It has no advertising code and no tracking code in it. We do not follow you across other apps or websites, and we do not build an advertising profile of you.

5 · If you worked with TAG before you had the app

If you have worked with TAG before, we may already hold your details, your cards and your documents from our own records. When your TAG Connect account is matched to those records, they appear in your profile.

We got that information from TAG’s own files and systems, not from a third party or a public source. You have all the rights in section 2 over it, exactly as if you had given it to us yourself.

7 · How your documents are read

When you photograph a card or an identity document, TAG’s own in-house software reads the details printed on it, so you do not have to type them. Here is exactly what happens:

  1. The photograph is stored securely on TAG’s servers.
  2. TAG’s software reads the printed details. Where any part of the service is run for us by a provider, they act under contract and only on our instructions, and may not use your documents for their own purposes or to train their systems on them.
  3. A member of TAG staff then looks at it. They see your photograph alongside what the software read and how confident it was, so anything read wrongly is corrected.
  4. Only then does anything appear as verified on your profile.

The photograph is what counts. The text read from it is for convenience. If something was read wrongly, tell us and we will fix it.

We use the documents you upload and the corrections our team makes to keep TAG’s own software accurate. Your documents stay yours, and this never shares them beyond what this policy allows.

8 · Verified, Skill Score and Activity

Your profile carries three things that recruiters can see:

  • Verified — whether TAG has checked your identity and your right to work.
  • Skill Score — points worked out from the qualifications we have checked. It uses the grade printed on your card and never guesses one.
  • Activity — how recently you have used TAG Connect.

When a job comes in, TAG’s own software suggests workers whose qualifications, skills, area, availability, Verified status and past placements fit it. It does not use your age, date of birth, nationality, language, sex or name to do this.

This is profiling in law, so you should know:

  • A recruiter always decides who is put forward and who is placed. A member of TAG staff always decides whether you are Verified.
  • No decision that affects you legally or similarly significantly is made by the app on its own.
  • If you think a status or a score is wrong, ask us and a person will look at it. Section 2 tells you how.
  • Your score goes up as you add and renew documents.

9 · The photograph of your face

When you verify your identity you take a photograph of yourself.

  • A member of TAG staff compares it with the photograph on your document. We do not use face recognition software.
  • The photograph is deleted within 90 days of the check.
  • The document images and this photograph are held for reference. They are not the legal right to work check — a member of TAG staff does that by looking at your original document.

11 · Where your information is kept

Your profile, your documents and your photographs are held on TAG’s own servers. Only TAG, and the companies that run parts of the service for us under contract, can reach them.

Some technical services that deliver messages and notifications to your phone operate internationally. Only what is needed to deliver the message reaches them, and it is protected by safeguards approved under UK law. You can ask us for details.

13 · How we protect it

Your information is encrypted and kept on secure servers, and only staff who need it can see it. Personal details are hidden by default on our screens, and revealed only when there is a reason — which is recorded. We never email your documents in plain text. If a breach ever put you at risk, we would tell you and the ICO, as the law requires.

14 · What is stored on your phone

The app keeps your sign-in session in your phone’s secure storage — the Keychain on iPhone, the Keystore on Android — and keeps a copy of your home screen so it loads quickly.

There are no advertising cookies and no tracking code in this app.

The app asks for:

  • your camera, to photograph cards and documents
  • your photos, if you want to upload a picture you already have
  • notifications, so we can tell you when a document is verified or is about to expire

You can refuse or withdraw any of these in your phone’s settings. The app does not ask for your location.

15 · Messages we send you

To run the service we send you sign-in codes by text message and notifications about your documents and your work. These are part of the service and you cannot turn them off while you have an account, though you can control how they appear on your phone.

Marketing is separate. We only send it if you choose it, and you can stop it at any time without it affecting anything else.

16 · Age

TAG Connect is for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.
