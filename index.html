TAG Connect · Privacy Policy

Your data, your control

This policy explains how TAG Construction Limited collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use the TAG Connect app.

Version 1.1 · Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

TAG Construction Limited is the data controller for TAG Connect — the company responsible for collecting, storing, and protecting your personal information in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We are registered in England, registered office 55 Bath Street, Gravesend, Kent, DA11 0DF.

What we collect

We collect:

  • your name, email, contact number, date of birth, address, and National Insurance number
  • photos of identity documents (passport, driving licence), your profile photo and a selfie, which a TAG team member checks against your photo ID. We don't use automatic face matching.
  • qualification cards and certificates (CSCS, CPCS, NPORS and others)
  • health-related certificates where your work requires them — for example occupational health or drug-and-alcohol test certificates (see “Health-related documents” below)
  • work history, site attendance and deployment records, and site diary entries
  • messages you exchange with recruiters in the app
  • emergency contact details
  • technical information — device type, app version, sign-in and usage logs — which we use to keep the service secure and working

We only collect what we need.

Two things worth knowing: your emergency contact is another person’s information — please make sure they’re happy for you to share it. And a recruiter can sometimes upload a document on your behalf (for example, a card you handed over in person); when that happens, the document appears in your wallet and is treated exactly as if you uploaded it yourself.

How your documents are processed

Card and ID images are read by automated document-reading (AI) systems to extract the details printed on them. Extraction is automated, but verification decisions are made by people — a recruiter reviews your documents before anything appears as verified on your profile. The card image itself is the source of truth; extracted text is for convenience, and you can ask us to correct anything that was read wrongly.

The AI reading is performed by specialist AI providers acting under contract as our processors. This can involve your document images being processed on servers inside the UK; where that happens, the transfer is protected and the providers may not use your documents for their own purposes.

To keep the reading accurate, we use the documents you upload and the corrections you or our team make to operate and improve our document-reading systems. This never changes who owns your documents, and it never shares them beyond what this policy and your sharing choices allow.

Health-related documents

Some cards workers upload — occupational health certificates, drug-and-alcohol test results, medicals — contain health information, which the law treats with extra protection (“special category data”). We process these only where they are required for your work on site, relying on the employment-law condition under UK data protection law, and we hold them to the same security standard as everything else, with access limited to those who need it for compliance.

Why we collect it — and our legal grounds

Your data is used to manage your employment or deployment, verify your qualifications and right to work, track your site attendance, ensure health and safety compliance, and process your pay. UK GDPR requires us to name the legal ground for each purpose:

  • Verifying your documents, managing your deployment, running your wallet and profile, processing your pay — performing our contract with you.
  • Right-to-work checks, HMRC and HSE reporting, health & safety records, record-keeping the law requires — legal obligation.
  • Showing your wallet to recruiters and sites you apply to or are deployed to — your consent, given when you set up your account and withdrawable any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Health-related certificates required for site work — the employment-law condition (special category data).
  • Keeping the service secure, preventing fraud, fixing problems, improving how the app and our document-reading work — our legitimate interests, balanced against your rights.

If you don’t provide the basics — identity, right to work, required cards — we can’t verify you, and recruiters can’t deploy you. That’s not a penalty; it’s what the verification service is.

We do not sell your data. Ever.

Your status

TAG recruiters see your name, trade, cards, status and contact details so they can offer you work. Your ID, share code, selfie and nationality are only used to check who you are and that you can work in the UK. Your NI number is only used for pay.

Your status (for example “Verified”) shows whether TAG has checked your ID and right to work. Every verification decision is made by a person at TAG. No hiring or pay decision is made by the app itself — those are made by the employers and recruiters you deal with.

How it's protected

Your data is stored encrypted on secure servers, with access restricted to authorised personnel only. We use industry-standard security and conduct regular data protection audits. Your documents are never emailed in plain text. If a data breach ever put your rights at risk, we would tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

Who we share with

Your profile is private by default. No company or recruiter can see your data unless you apply to or are deployed to them. We may share relevant data with:

  • client sites and recruiters you are deployed to or apply to (e.g. name, qualifications, right-to-work status)
  • regulatory bodies where legally required (e.g. HMRC, Health and Safety Executive)
  • payroll providers, to process your pay
  • service providers who run parts of the platform for us under contract and on our instructions — cloud hosting, SMS and notification delivery, and the AI document-reading providers described above

We never share more than necessary.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an active account (in line with UK employment law). Financial records are kept for 7 years (HMRC requirement). Two categories work slightly differently: audit records of verification and compliance decisions are kept for the same statutory periods even if you close your account, and the record of the consents you gave or withdrew is kept as evidence of those consents. After the retention periods, your data is securely deleted. You can request deletion at any time — see Your rights below.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct errors
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent
  • Object to processing

Withdrawing consent will not affect anything we processed before you withdrew it. You can withdraw at any time in Settings → Privacy.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. See Delete your account for what is deleted and what the law makes us keep.

How to exercise your rights

To make a request, ask a question, or raise a concern about your data, contact:

Storage on your phone

The app stores essential information on your device — your sign-in session and your settings — so it works properly. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Age

TAG Connect is for people aged 18 or over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you believe we have, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example when we add features or the law changes. If a change is material we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The version line at the top shows when we last revised it.
