Common questions

Who can use TAG Connect?

Anyone looking for construction work can download the app and sign up with their mobile number. You don't need to have worked for TAG before.

How do I sign in?

Enter your mobile number and we'll text you a one-time code. There's no password to remember.

How long does verification take?

Within 2 working days, usually the same day.

My card isn't being read properly

Use good light and place the card on a flat surface. If it still doesn't work, contact us and we'll help.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my account. You can also read how account deletion works, including what the law makes us keep.

Report a problem

Email support@tagconstructionltd.co.uk with the subject "Bug Report - TAG Connect". Tell us what happened and which phone you use. Please don't send photos of your ID or cards by email.
